Part 2: Prediction

Reading Skill: Prediction

Good readers make predictions before and during reading. A prediction is a smart guess based on the title, pictures, story clues, and what we already know.

Predictions help us focus, look for important clues, and change our thinking when we get new information.

Look at the Clues

Clue 1

The title is The Birds of Killingsworth.
Clue 2

The townspeople think birds are causing trouble.
Clue 3

Birds often eat insects, seeds, or fruit.

Which prediction is BEST?

The birds will teach the townspeople how to build houses.
The townspeople may learn that the birds are more helpful than they thought.
The story will only explain how birds fly.

Part 3: Reading Skill Prep — Cause & Effect

Reading Skill: Cause & Effect

A cause is why something happens. An effect is what happens because of the cause.

When we read, we can ask:
  • Why did this happen?
  • What happened because of this?
  • Did one choice create a new problem?

Practice

Cause: The birds are driven away.

What is the most likely effect?

The town has more music.
There may be more insects because fewer birds are eating them.
The birds build a school.

Now Read the Story

Read The Birds of Killingsworth. While reading, look for:
  • what problem the townspeople notice
  • what decision they make
  • what happens after the birds are gone
  • what lesson the town learns

Part 4: After-Reading Quick Check

After Reading Strategy: Check and Think Again

After we read, good readers stop and check their understanding. We ask: What happened?, Why did it happen?, and What lesson can I infer?

Part 5: Cause → Effect Match

Choose one cause and one matching effect.

Causes

The townspeople believe the birds are hurting their crops.
The birds are driven away from Killingsworth.
The people finally notice what happens without birds.

Effects

Insects can become a bigger problem for the town.
The people begin to understand that birds are useful.
They decide to get rid of the birds.

Part 6: Theme Practice

Reading Skill: Theme

The theme is the message or lesson in a story. It is not just what happened. It is what the story teaches us about life, choices, or people.

Which theme best fits this story?

Birds should never live near people.
People should think carefully before changing nature, because every living thing may have an important role.
Crops are always more important than animals.

Part 7: Worksheet Preparation

Your worksheet may ask you to explain causes, effects, and the lesson of the story. A strong answer should include:
  • your idea
  • a story clue
  • your explanation

Choose the strongest worksheet answer.

The story is about birds. Birds are nice.
The people learned that the birds were important because after the birds were gone, the town had new problems. This shows that nature is connected.
The people did not like birds, so the birds left.
